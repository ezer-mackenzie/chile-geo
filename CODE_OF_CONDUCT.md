# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, welcome constructive disagreement, give actionable feedback, respect privacy, and focus discussion on the project. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and whenever someone publicly represents the project. Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, and the safety of affected participants.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
